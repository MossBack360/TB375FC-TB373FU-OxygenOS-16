## 2026-11-17 attention clock correction

The owner clarified they were away during the zero-face samples. ASI later detected face and gaze, and the helper successfully updated `PowerManager.userActivity()`.

The long-uptime bug was the timeout calculation. `lastUserActivityTime` uses Android `SystemClock.uptimeMillis()` (excluding deep sleep), while `/proc/uptime` includes suspended time. A pre-fix sample showed `/proc/uptime=30737 s` versus `lastUserActivityTime=14275020 ms (16269 ms ago)`. The old subtraction made `remaining` deeply negative after suspend and stopped attention checks. v0.3 parses the `(... ms ago)` age from the same `dumpsys power` line and computes `remaining=timeout-age`; request throttling uses wall seconds separately.
