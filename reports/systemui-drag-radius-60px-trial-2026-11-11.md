# SystemUI bottom-corner drag radius trial

The owner asked to test values other than the old v0.5 fallback of approximately 30px at 400dpi. The currently running framework reports `Display.getRoundedCorner()` as zero because the experimental RRO is disabled. The old v0.5 `FullscreenTaskImageView.getCornerRadius()` computes `dpToPx(max((int)(displayRadiusPx / density) - 8, 12))`, so at zero it returns 12dp = 30px.

An initial Vector/LSPosed experiment was built under `patch/drag-radius-hook/` to intercept the stock SystemUI drag path and read `debug.fixo.drag_radius_px`. Vector CLI showed the module enabled and scoped to SystemUI and logged that its class loaded, but no module callback or target-method hit was observed after three builds and restarts. The APK was disabled in Vector and uninstalled. Do not claim this hook worked.

The tested successor uses the proven SystemUI v0.5 APK path. Builder `patch/build_systemui_drag_radius_trial.py` accepts a pixel radius from 0 to 200 and replaces the single `SpringDragManager.handleMoveInAnim()` radius-source instruction sequence with `const/16 v4, radiusPx` plus NOP padding, preserving the DEX instruction span. Its source is the exact v0.5 APK SHA-256 `92c57e647c9520cb043be1d0a0cf010c6f1e7dec2b72129837b45cc261e57560`; only `classes2.dex` changes in the APK. The 60px APK is SHA-256 `74063211a9b3b59598655f7a1c0d6b1644e04e05e9aa0db67e2f7aaa58eb6a01`, and its module ZIP is SHA-256 `a90e309eb90d8a4c36a95cdea5ed4636166142de796346ff1dd181e186d442aa`. Files: `out/systemui-drag-radius-v0.6-60px-test-2026-11-11/`.

Focused JADX of the output shows `float f = 60` at the start of `SpringDragManager.handleMoveInAnim()`, followed by `mRadius = max(f * max(scaleX, scaleY), max(minScaleXInMove, minScaleYInMove) * f)` and `ReflectionHelper.setCornerRadiusExt(..., mRadius, 2.0f)`. Thus 60px is the base input, not necessarily every animation frame's final Surface radius.

Installed as an upgrade of Magisk module ID `fixo_fullscreen_caption` v0.6-60px-test. After reboot, the service logged `caption-overlay=ok pid=7557`, the mounted SystemUI APK hash matched the new payload, and PID 7557 remained stable. The older v0.5 ZIP remains preserved at `temp3/稳定安装/01-当前启用-Magisk/17-fullscreen-caption-systemui-v0.5.zip`. The 60px variant remained experimental until the owner's visual comparison below.

The owner subsequently confirmed the 60px drag preview was visibly rounder. On request, the same builder produced a 12px variant at `out/systemui-drag-radius-v0.6-12px-test-2026-11-11/`; APK SHA-256 `bfc03540057ce4870e6c5c1693ebbd7f5cb85383de0bfbb0cee51be186413eac`, ZIP SHA-256 `8c6c3379898edf6f36a12c5f0753f52c56c7f0f225dd0701f08c04feea09ed97`. This is 12 **px**, not the previous 12dp minimum (30px at 400dpi). After reboot, the service logged `caption-overlay=ok pid=7409`, mounted SystemUI matched the 12px APK hash, and PID 7409 stayed stable. Settings v5.3 remained mounted, SHA-256 `fe8b6161f642fbbacc5eb54646f5b5040afbfa93ca999de967f7013041369bcb`. Visual assessment of 12px is pending.
